Starter｜主詞、be、姓名與年齡

先分清楚「誰」和「誰的」。

1｜人稱 vs. 所有格

誰誰的例句
ImyMy name is Amy.
youyourWhat is your name?
hehisHis name is Tony.
sheherHer name is Judy.
ititsIts name is Lucky.
易錯：its = 它的；it's = it is。

Vocabulary｜單字速記

fine adj. 好的；健康的
How is she? → She is fine.
classmate n. 同班同學
We are classmates.
name n. 名字
What is her name?
old adj. ……歲的
How old is he?
數字拼字：twelve, thirteen, fourteen, eighteen, twenty, thirty, forty；21–99 非整十如 thirty-five 要加連字號。

2｜be 動詞

I am ｜ he / she / it is ｜ you / we / they are
She is thirteen.　We are classmates.

3｜問什麼，問句就不同

姓名

狀態

She / Her

Unit 1｜Who’s That Handsome Boy?

人物身分、親屬、職業、外貌、年齡都共用 be 句式。
Who｜人物
Who is that tall boy?
Family｜親屬
mother / uncle / cousin
Job｜職業
What is his job?

Vocabulary｜人物單字一起記

uncle n. 叔／伯／舅父
He’s my uncle, Paul.
cousin n. 堂／表兄弟姊妹
The handsome boy is my cousin.
husband n. 丈夫
husband and wife
daughter n. 女兒
His daughter is eight.
housewife n. 家庭主婦
police officer n. 警察
He is a police officer.
handsome adj. 英俊的
that handsome boy
beautiful adj. 美麗的
elementary adj. 小學的
an elementary school teacher
family n. 家庭；家人

1｜Who 問人物

Who is he?
He’s my uncle, Paul.

2｜形容詞位置

He is tall. ↔ He is a tall boy.
形容詞可在 be 後，也可放名詞前。

3｜年齡兩種寫法

He is eight years old.
He is an eight-year-old boy.
易錯：名詞前的 eight-year-old 要連字號，year 不加 s；eight 前用 an。

4｜所有格

意思寫法
共同的一個兒子Amy and Paul's son
兩人各自的書包Amy's and Paul's school bags
立即練習
Nina is eight years old. → Nina is girl.
答案
an eight-year-old

Unit 2｜Where Are My Pencils?

距離 × 數量決定指示詞；數量也決定 is / are。

Vocabulary｜物品、房間與位置

bathroom n. 浴室

under the sofa

1｜this / that / these / those

近遠
單數this + isthat + is
複數these + arethose + are
先看 ONE 還是 MANY
↓
ONE → this / that → is
MANY → these / those → are
近＋單數
What is this? → This is an apple.
遠＋複數
What are those? → They are watches.

2｜a / an 與複數

不能：These are a new pencil cases. 複數前不能放 a / an。
box → boxes ｜ baby → babies ｜ woman → women

3｜Where 問位置

Where is the cat?
It is under the sofa.
Where are the watches?
They are by the door.
on在……上
under在……下
next to / by在……旁
between A and B在 A、B 之間
立即練習
近處兩本 notebooks：What these? → They notebooks.
答案
are / are
